01 public ledger [ every block · forever ]

every chain is an open diary.

every transaction, balance, and counterparty on a public chain is permanent, linkable, and readable by anyone — forever. the ledger never forgets.

02 encrypted [ Nox · euint256 ]

the encrypted layer forgets.

deposit into Wall and your balance becomes an encrypted handle — an euint256 inside a TEE. amount and owner stay inside the enclave; only you hold the decryption key.

03 auditable [ ACL · selective disclosure ]

the TEE is the lock.

every move is computed inside Nox's trusted execution environment. an auditor can decrypt via ACL — but only with your permission. no leak, no link, no trace.
